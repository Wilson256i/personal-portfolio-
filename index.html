01About

Quiet by nature. Deliberate by practice.

I’m Wilson — a front-end developer with full-stack range, based in Hoima City, Uganda. I care about the part of a product people actually touch: the layout, the rhythm, and the way a screen responds when someone uses it.

I work slowly enough to get the details right, and quickly enough to keep a project moving. Most of what I do is invisible in the best way: interfaces that feel effortless, backed by code that holds up.

“Good interfaces don’t ask to be noticed. They just work.”

02Origin

It started with a blank page.

In 2016 I watched a blank page turn into something that moved and responded — and quietly decided that was the work I wanted to do.

Everything since comes back to that moment: take something empty, study it, and shape it until it feels alive.

03Craft

What I bring to a build.

Front-end 01

The part you touch

Layout, type, colour, and the small interactions that make a screen feel effortless. Responsive, accessible, and built to hold up across devices.

Full-stack 02

The part you don’t see

APIs, data, and the quiet engine behind the interface. I can carry a feature from the first sketch through to the server that powers it.

Motion & detail 03

The part you feel

Timing, easing, and restraint. Motion used as a manner, not decoration — enough to guide, never enough to distract.

The goal is simple: make it feel inevitable.
